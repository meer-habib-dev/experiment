# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, specific, and generous in technical discussion. Welcome people with different
backgrounds and experience levels. Critique the work, not the person, and assume good intent while
still addressing harmful behavior directly.

Harassment, discrimination, sexualized attention, threats, doxxing, and sustained disruption are
not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when behavior harms the community. Reports should be sent privately through the
security contact method documented in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); do not open a public issue for a
sensitive conduct report.

Maintainers will protect reporters' privacy as far as reasonably possible and will respond in a
way proportionate to the circumstances.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project.
It is adapted from the principles of the Contributor Covenant without reproducing its full text.
