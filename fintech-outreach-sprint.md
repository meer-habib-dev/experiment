# Fintech mobile client sprint

## Positioning

Use this sentence everywhere:

> I build high-performance fintech apps with Expo and React Native, including native iOS modules, biometric flows, real-time interfaces, Skia graphics, polished motion, and App Store delivery.

Do not lead with “full-stack developer” or “I can build any app.” The narrower message is more credible and maps directly to funded fintech needs.

## Revenue target

The fastest realistic paths to $5k/month are:

1. One full-time or long-term contract at $5k+/month.
2. One anchor client at $3k-$4k/month plus one maintenance client at $1k-$2k/month.
3. Two product sprints each month at $2.5k-$3.5k.

Avoid trying to manage three brand-new builds at once.

## Offers

### Paid mobile audit — $750-$1,250

Five business days:

- review performance, navigation, crashes, release setup, security-sensitive storage, and UX friction;
- deliver a prioritized report and one implemented high-impact fix;
- credit the audit fee toward a larger engagement.

### Two-week fintech product sprint — $2,500-$4,000

- one production-quality flow such as onboarding/KYC, wallet funding, portfolio charts, card controls, or transaction history;
- analytics, error states, loading states, and handoff documentation;
- a recorded demo and a scoped next phase.

### Fractional mobile lead — $3,000-$6,000/month

- two or three days per week;
- architecture, delivery, App Store releases, performance, and mentoring;
- best for a web-first team preparing a mobile product or a team whose existing app needs stronger ownership.

## Seven-day execution

### Day 1

- publish one 60-90 second Native Lab demo;
- make a one-page portfolio with three capabilities: real-time finance UI, secure native integrations, and polished product UX;
- submit Dreamcash, Fin, Pave Bank, and Vested applications.

### Day 2

- send personalized messages to Mal, Daya, and Seapoint;
- apply to Finbuddy with a one-page architecture proposal;
- publish one technical post showing an interaction, profiling result, or native module.

### Days 3-7

- add five newly funded or actively hiring leads per day;
- send five highly researched messages per day;
- follow up once after three business days with a useful artifact, not “just checking in”;
- aim for 25 quality contacts, five replies, and two calls in the first week.

Track: company, person, signal, message sent, follow-up date, reply, call, offer, next action.

## Rules

- Use only publicly provided business channels.
- Never guess email addresses.
- Mention one real product observation in every message.
- Ask for a call or a paid sprint, not “any opportunity.”
- Do not build unpaid full prototypes. A small screen mockup or 60-second teardown is enough.
